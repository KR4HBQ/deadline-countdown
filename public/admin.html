Publishing your changes

  1. Click Download config.js.
  2. Replace the config.js next to index.html on your site with it. On GitHub, open the repository, click config.js, then the pencil (or "Add file → Upload files") and commit.
  3. Reload the main page. GitHub Pages can take a minute to update.

Your edits are kept in this browser as a draft until you discard them, so you can close this page and come back. The password keeps casual visitors out of this page. It doesn't secure anything, since anyone can read a static site's files. What actually protects your countdowns is who can change the files on your host.
